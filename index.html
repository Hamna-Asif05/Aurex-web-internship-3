About

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of the Punjab, currently deep in a structured web development track at NAVTECH. I like projects where the front end and back end are designed together — where a layout decision on the client side and a schema decision on the server side are really the same decision.

Most of what I build lives in React and Node, with MySQL or Supabase underneath. On the design side, I care about the same things I'm practicing here: grid systems, restrained motion, and interfaces that don't need explaining.
